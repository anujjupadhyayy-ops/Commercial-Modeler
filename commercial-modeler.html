How to use this — 30-second orientation
This is a live sample model of a consulting deal. Everything on screen is editable and pre-filled with example numbers — nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The summary strip at the top is the punchline: deal value, margin, win probability, and a Pursue / Qualify / Drop call.
  • Switch the pricing model (Fixed / T&M / Capped) and watch how delivery-overrun risk shifts between provider and client.
  • Edit any resource line — days, cost/day, margin% — and add phases; the sell rate and margins recompute live.
  • Drag Win probability to see the recommendation move (win-likelihood is judged separately from the economics).
  • Add a 3PP item to see third-party pass-through dilute blended margin below services margin.
  • Flip to Portfolio (top left) to aggregate deals and see each one's marginal effect.
